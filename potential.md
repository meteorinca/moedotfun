Here are fresh ideas that fit that exact vibe and can each live as a self-contained static demo under `demos/`:

### 1. Viral Toys & Absurd Mini-Challenges

- **Draw a Perfect Circle** — One-stroke canvas challenge with live radial-error scoring, heat-mapped deviation highlights, personal best tracking, and snarky rank titles (*"Compass"*, *"Potato"*, *"Modern Art"*).
- **The Infinite Button** — A single tempting red button that escalates through absurd phases every click: it runs away from your cursor, clones itself into decoys, asks passive-aggressive confirmation modals, turns into a mini boss fight, and tracks your "patience score."

### 2. Scroll & Scale Explorers *(companion pieces to Deep Sea & Time Progress)*
- **Space Elevator** — The upward twin to *The Deep Sea Explorer*. Scroll from sea level through clouds, Everest, the Kármán line, ISS, satellites, the Moon, and deep space, with live altitude, gravity, and temperature gauges.
- **Size of the Microverse** — Pinch/slider zoom from a human hair down to red blood cells, viruses, DNA, silicon transistors (3nm!), atoms, protons, and the Planck length.
- **Internet in One Second** — A live ticking dashboard visualizing how many emails, YouTube hours, Google searches, GitHub commits, and terabytes of traffic have happened *since you opened the page*.

### 3. Nerdy CS, Physics & Math Sandboxes *(fits Coin Flip / Entropy / Logic Gates)*
- **Falling Sand Alchemy** — A silky-smooth cellular automata canvas with sand, water, fire, acid, plant, stone, electricity, and gunpowder — plus a few secret reactions (e.g. sand + lightning = glass).
- **Sorting Algorithm Soundboard** — Watch *and hear* Bubble, Quick, Merge, Radix, and Bogo Sort race side by side using Web Audio synth tones, with custom array shapes (sorted, reversed, pyramid, chaotic).
- **Gravity Golf / Orbital Slingshot** — Launch a probe around planets and black holes to hit a target orbit. Shows live predicted trajectory paths and lets you place your own planets.
